---

## What the dashboard does

Mashroo3i helps you answer questions like:

- Who is applying?
- Which sectors are most popular?
- How many applicants are accepted?
- How do Arabic and English cohorts compare?
- What kinds of people are applying — individuals, teams, students, or
  experienced professionals?
- How do outcomes change over time?

You can explore the whole picture in one place, then narrow things down with
the sidebar filters by year, cohort, outcome, sector, and applicant type.

---

## What you'll find inside

| Page | What it shows |
| --- | --- |
| **Overview** | A quick look at applications over time, cohort mix, applicant types, nationalities, and team sizes |
| **Applicant Profile** | Background details such as age, education, business stage, employment status, and outcomes |
| **Sectors & Applicant Type** | Popular sectors, individual vs. team applications, and how each group performs |
| **Cohort Comparison** | Arabic and English cohorts side by side, including outcomes and acceptance trends |
| **Attendance** | Member participation by cohort, year, and sector (when an attendance file is uploaded) |

## A little extra help from AI

Three built-in tools go beyond the charts:

### ✅ Idea Validator

Describe an idea in plain language and get a research-backed review. The
dashboard scores it against the official selection criteria, explains the
reasoning, highlights risks, and notes its possible impact on Bahrain.

### 🎯 Selection Advisor

When the full applicant file is uploaded, the dashboard creates a shortlist of
the most promising candidates, then reviews the top ones in more detail — so
you can see both the ranking and the story behind each candidate.

### 🔍 Similarity & Search

Spot ideas that are very similar to past submissions, browse related groups,
or search the idea archive by any topic. It's a useful way to avoid repeats
before selection.
